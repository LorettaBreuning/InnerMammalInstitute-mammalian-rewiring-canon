# Influence Protocol (Applied)

**Purpose:** Applies the Mammalian Re-Wiring Protocol™ to parenting, education, and leadership. Replaces emotional validation with observable wiring mechanics.

**Core Mechanics:**
- Identify the wiring built from the subject's past experience.
- Withhold rewards for diversionary tactics or cortisol explosions.
- Deliver small rewards for forward steps.
- Allow natural consequences to trigger cortisol so the nervous system learns redirection.

**Routing (Walt):** Offers should be structured around the three-step protocol. Avoid bundling with therapeutic, wellness, or recovery paradigms. Pricing and positioning reflect skill acquisition, not emotional support.
