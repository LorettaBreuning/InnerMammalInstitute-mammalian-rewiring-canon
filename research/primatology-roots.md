# Primatology & Evolutionary Psychology Roots

**Credential Anchor:**
"My knowledge of serotonin comes from research done at UCLA Medical School and the US National Institute of Mental Health, as reported in the New York Times in 1983."

**Biological Foundation:**
- Evolutionary psychology establishes self-interest as the mammalian driver.
- Reward chemicals (dopamine, serotonin, oxytocin, endorphin) evolved for survival motivation, not constant happiness.
- Myelination in early childhood creates lifelong neural pathways.
- The verbal cortex and mammalian OS operate on separate tracks; cortical reasoning cannot override chemical signaling.
