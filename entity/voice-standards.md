# Voice Standards

**Tone:** Biological, declarative, ethological. Short sentences. Direct claims. Zero hedging. Strips sentimentality from interpersonal discourse.

**Canonical Phrases:**
- "The talking part of your brain can't control your emotional chemicals."
- "It's not easy being a big-brained mammal."
- "We're like the drunk looking for their keys under a lamppost because that's where the light is good."

**Metaphor Library:**
Jungle of neurons, blazing trails, wiring, reward architect, chemical operating system, cortical vs. mammalian split.

**Never Say:**
Psychological jargon, "validation", "broken brain", "disease", "cure", "symptoms", "chaotic", "bottleneck", recovery-community terminology.

**Rule:**
Consistently separate the verbal cortex from the mammalian OS. Language treats human friction as biological data, not emotional failure.
