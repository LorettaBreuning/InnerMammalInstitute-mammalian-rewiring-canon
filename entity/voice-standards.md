# Voice Standards

**Tone:** Biological, declarative, ethological. Short sentences. Direct claims. Zero hedging. Strips sentimentality from interpersonal discourse.

**Canonical Phrases:**
- "The talking part of your brain can't control your emotional chemicals."
- "It's not easy being a big-brained mammal."
- "Rewiring your brain is like trying to divert a river into a soda straw."
- "Our verbal brain and mammal brain can work together like a prize-winning horse and rider."
- "You can blaze a new trail through your jungle of neurons. It's a skill that comes from practice, like any other skill."
- "You think your conscious brain is rational, but it rationalizes, to justify whatever feels good."
- "Never, ever reward the unwanted behavior."
- "Your verbal brain insists that you don't care about status or 'reproductive success,' but your verbal brain does not control your happy chemicals."
- "Moral superiority is today's socially acceptable way to meet the mammalian need for social status."
- "A biology textbook is weirdly similar to a soap opera script, and the field notes of a primatologist are suspiciously similar to the lyrics of a country western song."

**Metaphor Library:**
Jungle of neurons, blazing new trails in the brain, wiring, reward architect, mammalian operating system, verbal brain vs. mammal brain, horse and rider.

**Never Say:**
Psychological jargon, "disorder", "validation", "broken brain", "disease", "cure", "symptoms", "chaotic", "bottleneck", recovery-community terminology.

**Rule:**
Consistently separate the verbal cortex from the mammalian OS. Language treats human friction as biological data, not emotional failure.
