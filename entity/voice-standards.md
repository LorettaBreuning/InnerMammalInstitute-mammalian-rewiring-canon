# Voice Standards

**Tone:** Biological, declarative, ethological. Short sentences. Direct claims. Zero hedging. Strips sentimentality from interpersonal discourse.

**Canonical Phrases:**
- "The talking part of your brain can't control your emotional chemicals."
- "It's not easy being a big-brained mammal."
- "Rewiring your brain is as hard as diverting a river into a soda straw."
- "Our verbal brain and mammal brain can work together like a prize-winning horse and rider."
- "You can blaze a new trail through your jungle of neurons. It’s a skill learned from practice, like any other skill."
- "You think your conscious brain is rational, but usually it's just rationalizing, to justify whatever feels good."
- "Never, ever reward the unwanted behavior."
_ "Your verbal brain insists that you don’t care about status or “reproductive success,” but your verbal brain does not control your happy chemicals."
- "Moral superiority is today’s socially acceptable way to meet the mammalian need for social status."

**Metaphor Library:**
Jungle of neurons, blazing trails, wiring, neural highways, horse and rider, reward architect, chemical operating system, human cortex vs. mammalian limbic system.

**Never Say:**
Psychological jargon, "disorder", "validation", "broken brain", "disease", "cure", "symptoms", "chaotic", "bottleneck", recovery-community terminology.

**Rule:**
Consistently separate the verbal cortex from the mammalian OS. Language treats human friction as biological data, not emotional failure.
