# Soul Client Definition

**Profile:** Universal biological baseline, with highest resonance for adults trapped in status anxiety/cortisol spirals, and for those seeking a biological protocol to influence others (parents, educators, leaders) without relying on emotional validation.

**Core Friction:**
"The verbal brain does not control the emotional chemicals... Our verbal brain has been trained to believe that our mammalian impulses are bad, so it denies them... We see the mammalian self-interest in others but we don't see it in ourselves."

**Soul Client Language:**
- Wants agency, accountability to reality, practical protocols.
- Wants to feel good.
- Rejects victimhood narratives.
- Responds to biological causality over psychological comfort.

**Routing (Carl):** Codex should map the exact friction points of the status-anxiety loop and the reward-architect transition.
